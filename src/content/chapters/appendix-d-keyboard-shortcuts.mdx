This appendix lists the keyboard shortcuts supported in **APS ELN Community** and **APS ELN ("For Teams")** (<a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}/ch3-a-tour-of-the-main-window#the-dashboard-and-keyboard-navigation`}>Chapter 3: The Dashboard and keyboard navigation</a>). Shortcuts that operate on a specific panel require that panel to have keyboard focus and are inactive while you are typing inside a search box or text field.

## Global and main window shortcuts

<a href="#table-d-1">Table D-1</a> lists the global navigation and window shortcuts. Note where **APS ELN Community** and the **Commercial ("For Teams")** edition assign different actions to `Ctrl+D` and `F5`.

<p id="table-d-1" class="table-caption"><strong>Table D-1:</strong> Global and main window shortcuts.</p>

<DataTable
  headers={['Shortcut', 'Action in APS ELN Community', 'Action in Team Lab (Commercial)']}
  rows={[
    ['`F1`', '**Help > Keyboard Shortcuts...**', '**Help > Keyboard Shortcuts...**'],
    ['`Ctrl+F`', '**Navigate > Search...** (notebook search)', 'Full-text / semantic **Search** across projects'],
    ['`Ctrl+G`', '**Navigate > Go to Item...** (by ID or `eln://` URL)', '**Go to item** by ID or `eln://` URL'],
    ['`Ctrl+D`', '**Navigate > Dashboard**', 'Duplicates the selected item when the **Items** panel has focus (use **Edit > Dashboard** for the Dashboard)'],
    ['`F5`', 'Not bound (use **Refresh** in the menu or toolbar)', 'Refreshes the active panel'],
    ['`Ctrl+E`', 'Use **File > Export...** from the menu', '**File > Export Package**'],
    ['`Ctrl+Shift+A`', 'Use **Show Archived** in the Projects panel', 'Toggles archived projects visibility'],
    ['`Ctrl+Shift+C`', 'Not bound', 'Copies the selected file path'],
    ['`Ctrl+Shift+R`', 'Use **Tools > Protocol Review...** from the menu', 'Opens the **Protocol Review Queue**'],
  ]}
/>
