Not every piece of laboratory knowledge belongs inside a single deposition or measurement action. Project goals, literature reviews, substrate-cleaning standard operating procedures, and cross-sample comparisons span an entire project. At the same time, individual items often need quick visual badges (`Tags`), team discussion threads (`Comments`), or explicit links to the target or substrate batch they came from (`Links`). This chapter shows how to use all four tools without cluttering your experimental tree (<a href="#fig-11-1">Figure 11-1</a>).

## The five-section Project Wiki

A **Wiki page** in APS ELN is an item of type **Wiki** whose body is a Markdown (`.md`) file stored right inside your notebook's attachment directory. To keep project knowledge organized across every project and every student, APS ELN enforces a standard five-section folder structure under `Wiki/` (<a href="#fig-11-1">Figure 11-1</a>):

- **`Wiki / Goals`:** project objectives, grant milestones, and target specifications.
- **`Wiki / Ideas`:** design calculations, brainstorming notes, and preliminary sketches.
- **`Wiki / Literature`:** summaries of published papers, reference parameters, and competing structures.
- **`Wiki / Analysis`:** cross-experiment write-ups, summary tables, and comparative plots.
- **`Wiki / References`:** instrument manuals, calibration certificates, and standard operating procedures (SOPs).

Follow these steps to create and edit a project Wiki:

1. Select your project (or a folder inside it) in the **Projects** panel and choose **Project > New Wiki** (or click **New Topic Wiki** on the toolbar). Confirm **Add a Wiki to ...?**. APS ELN applies the `Wiki` template and creates the `Wiki` folder with its five section subfolders.
2. To add a new page inside one of the five sections, open that section folder (such as `Wiki / Analysis`), click **New** (`Ins`), select **Wiki** as the item type, enter the page **Title**, and click **Save**.
3. Single-click the Wiki item in the **Items** panel to read its rendered HTML in the **Markdown** viewer.
4. Click **Edit** in the viewer toolbar to open the **Markdown Editor** (<a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}/ch7-raw-files-drafts-and-instrument-viewers#working-drafts-external-edits-and-conflict-resolution`}>Chapter 7: Working drafts, external edits, and conflict resolution</a>), write your Markdown text, and press `Ctrl+S` (or click **Save**).

To include a figure, diagram, or PDF inside a Wiki page, drag the local file into the open **Markdown Editor** (or copy an image to the clipboard and press `Ctrl+V`). APS ELN uploads the file as an attachment of that Wiki item and inserts the Markdown reference (`![Caption](figure.png)`) automatically.

<Remember>To keep the tree clean, a Wiki item hides its attached `.md` body and image files in the **Items** panel and hides the bottom thumbnail strip. In the item tree, **Item > Edit** edits the Wiki item's title and metadata; to edit the page text, always click the **Edit** button inside the **Markdown** viewer.</Remember>

<Warning title="dropped or pasted Wiki assets upload immediately">When you drag a file or press `Ctrl+V` to paste an image into the **Markdown Editor**, APS ELN uploads that asset to the Wiki item immediately so it can generate the Markdown link. Clicking **Cancel** on your text edit later does not delete the uploaded image attachment.</Warning>

## Navigating between Wiki pages and experimental records

Inside any Markdown Wiki page or attachment, you can create live links that jump directly to any item or specimen in your notebook (even across different projects):

- **Short bracket syntax:** write `[[item:4]]` to link to item ID `4`, or `[[specimen:261015A]]` to link to the specimen whose title is `261015A`.
- **Standard Markdown URL syntax:** write `[Indentation 261015A](eln://item/4)` or `[Sample 261015A](eln://specimen/261015A)`.

When you click the rendered link in the **Markdown** viewer, APS ELN navigates the **Projects** and **Items** panels straight to that record. You can also jump to any item ID or `eln://` URL at any time by choosing **Navigate > Go to Item...** (`Ctrl+G`).

## Classifying items with Tags and Favorites

While folders group items by location, **Tags** let you label items across different folders and projects (for example, `High-Hardness`, `Annealed`, `Thesis-Fig-3`, or `Repeat-Needed`):

1. Right-click an item (or select multiple items with `Ctrl`+click / `Shift`+click and right-click inside the selection) and choose **Tags...**.
2. Tick a tag's check box to assign it to the selected item(s), or clear the check box to remove it. When multiple items are selected, the **Items** column shows `k of N` (how many of the $N$ selected items currently carry that tag). Changes take effect immediately when you click the check box.
3. To create a new tag, click **Add**, enter **Name:**, pick a badge color with **Choose...**, and click **OK**.
4. Click **Close** when finished. Assigned tags appear as colored badges right beside the item title in the **Items** panel.

<Warning title="clicking Delete in the Tags dialog removes the tag from the whole notebook">In the **Tags...** dialog (and in **Reference Data > Tags...**), clicking the **Delete** button deletes that tag definition and strips it from every item in the notebook. To remove a tag from the selected item only, simply uncheck its box.</Warning>

To bookmark a project, folder, or item that you visit every day, right-click it and choose **Add to Favorites**. It appears under the **Favorites** branch in the left **Projects** panel for one-click access. Choosing **Remove from Favorites** removes the bookmark without touching the record.

## Recording Comments and typed item relationships

APS ELN provides two ways to attach prose to an item and two ways to link items together (<a href="#table-11-1">Table 11-1</a>):

- **Rich-text Notes (`Item > Add Note`):** formal experimental observations stored as child entries of the item and included in signed item hashes.
- **Comments (`Right-click > Comments...`):** a timestamped discussion thread where you and your supervisor can post questions or review notes. Select a comment you wrote and click **Edit** or **Delete** to revise or remove it; you can only edit your own comments.

When one scientific item depends on another item in a way that is not a parent-child tree relationship (for example, specimen `261015A` was deposited from a specific `B4C/BN Target` item, or a repeat run `261020A` duplicates `Deposition 261015A`), create a **typed item relationship**:

1. Right-click the target item in the **Items** panel and choose **Copy as Link Target**.
2. Right-click the source item and choose **Link to...**.
3. In the **Relationship:** drop-down, pick the relationship phrase (configured in **Reference Data > Link Types...**). Read the preview sentence in the dialog carefully to verify the direction (which item is the source and which is the target).
4. Enter optional **Notes:** and click **Link**.
5. To view, follow, edit, or remove relationships on an item, select the item and choose **Item > Links...** (or double-click an inline link row to jump to the linked item).
