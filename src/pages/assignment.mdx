## Step 1: Download the Raw Data Packages from the Lab Server

Download both raw instrument bundles from the CSMIC server and unpack them into two separate folders on your computer:

1. **Study A (`261015A`, BN/DLC tribological coating, 11 raw files):** <a href="http://10.109.92.41/MasteringELN/data/Study-A-261015A-raw.zip" download="Study-A-261015A-raw.zip">`http://10.109.92.41/MasteringELN/data/Study-A-261015A-raw.zip`</a>. This archive includes `.depo.json`, `.run.json`, `.tsv`, three step-height `.fibps` scans, `.mit` nanoindentation, `.zrw` friction test, three wear-track `.fibps` scans, and `checksums.md5`.
2. **Study B (`261014B`, Co/C multilayer X-ray mirror, 7 raw files):** <a href="http://10.109.92.41/MasteringELN/data/Study-B-261014B-raw.zip" download="Study-B-261014B-raw.zip">`http://10.109.92.41/MasteringELN/data/Study-B-261014B-raw.zip`</a>. This archive includes the 3 deposition files, 3 plasma-cleaning files, the raw reflectivity scan `XRR-1_Co-C(261014B).xrdml`, and `checksums.md5`. The `.xrcx` fit file is omitted so you can produce it by fitting the `.xrdml` curve in Task 2.

## Step 2: Create Your Practice Group and Practice Projects

Log into **APS ELN** (`ELabNotebook3.exe`) with your personal laboratory account. Do not record practice data inside shared production projects.

1. In the left **Projects** panel, choose **Project > New Group** (or right-click an empty area of the Projects panel and choose **New Group...**). Enter the exact group name `Practice` and click **OK** (<a href="/MasteringELN/ch5-projects-groups-and-folders#creating-sorting-and-moving-projects-and-folders">Chapter 5: Creating, sorting, and moving projects and folders</a>).
2. Right-click your new `Practice` group and choose **New Project...**. Create your first practice project with the exact title `Study A Practice` and click **Save**.
3. Right-click your `Practice` group a second time and choose **New Project...**. Create your second practice project with the exact title `Study B Practice` and click **Save**.

<Remember>In the Commercial team edition, project groups are managed in the Desktop window. Creating `Study B Practice` inside your `Practice` group before launching your AI agent ensures both projects sit inside `Practice` under your user account.</Remember>

## Step 3: Complete Task 1 and Task 2

### Task 1: Study A (`261015A`) in the Desktop GUI (`Study A Practice`)

Read <a href="/MasteringELN/ch1-what-an-eln-is-and-why-links-matter#the-four-links-in-every-laboratory-result">Chapter 1</a> through <a href="/MasteringELN/ch4-your-first-complete-sample-record#meet-study-a-sample-261015a">Chapter 4: Your First Sample Record in Twenty Minutes</a> and follow the four steps of Chapter 4 inside your `Study A Practice` project:

1. **Build the 6-item tree first:** `Deposition 261015A` (`Procedure`) -> `261015A` (`Specimen`) -> child actions `Thickness 261015A`, `Indentation 261015A`, and `Friction test 261015A` (with child action `Wear track 261015A` under `Friction test 261015A`).
2. **Attach the 11 raw files** from `Study-A-261015A-raw.zip` to their matching items (<a href="/MasteringELN/ch4-your-first-complete-sample-record#table-4-1">Table 4-1</a>).
3. **Extract all 23 `Reader` values** using **Extract Values** and **Extract values from 3 files...**. Store `12` on `Deposition 261015A`, `3` on `Thickness 261015A`, `2` on `Indentation 261015A`, `3` on `Friction test 261015A`, and `3` on `Wear track 261015A`.
4. **Compute the 1 `Derived` value** on `Friction test 261015A` with **Add > Derived Value...** (`wear_rate_reciprocating`, $K = 3.05 \times 10^{-7}\,\text{mm}^3/(\text{N}\cdot\text{mm})$). Then right-click `Deposition 261015A` and run **Check Value Sources...** to verify zero drift across all 24 values.

### Task 2: Study B (`261014B`) with Your AI Agent and `XRC_MCP` (`Study B Practice`)

Read <a href="/MasteringELN/ch17-connecting-an-ai-agent-to-your-notebook#what-the-eln-mcp-server-does">Chapter 17</a> through <a href="/MasteringELN/ch19-study-b-xrr-fitting-with-provenance#why-agent-written-records-need-the-value-route">Chapter 19: Study B: XRR Fitting with Provenance</a>. Copy the 7 raw files from `Study-B-261014B-raw.zip` into your configured `--upload-root` directory and use your AI agent (`ELNMCPServer.exe` + `XRC_MCP.exe`) inside your `Study B Practice` project:

1. **Build the tree and attach the 7 raw files (<a href="/MasteringELN/ch19-study-b-xrr-fitting-with-provenance#part-1-the-record-agent-builds-study-b-261014b">Chapter 19: Part 1</a>):** Have the agent create folder `261014B` inside `Study B Practice` and create `Deposition 261014B` (`Proc`), `Cleaning 261014B` (`Action`), `261014B` (`Specimen`), and `XRR-1 261014B` (`Action`). Next, have it upload the 7 raw files and store the 18 `Reader` values (`14` on `Deposition 261014B` and `4` on `Cleaning 261014B`) via `extract_file_key_values` and `add_values`.
2. **Fit `XRR-1_Co-C(261014B).xrdml` via `XRC_MCP` (<a href="/MasteringELN/ch19-study-b-xrr-fitting-with-provenance#part-2-fitting-agent-records-xrr-fit-261014b">Chapter 19: Part 2</a>):** Have the agent fit the reflectivity curve (`SodaLimeGlass` / $[\text{C}/\text{Co}] \times 20$ / surface $\text{C}$ layer, polynomial order 1) in `XRC_MCP.exe`. Save the accepted `.xrcx` project using `curves.job_id` so `[FITRESULT]` and $\chi^2$ are written into the archive.
3. **Upload the `.xrcx` fit and store the 4 fit values:** Have the agent create child action `XRR fit 261014B` under `XRR-1 261014B` and upload the saved `.xrcx` file. Finally, have it extract and store the 4 `Reader` values (`Period`, `Co Thickness`, `C Thickness`, and `Chi2`) via `aps.xrcx`.

<Warning title="never type Manual values to pass the check">Every stored number in both projects is checked for `Reader` or `Derived` provenance. Typing a number by hand (`Manual` source) or saving an `.xrcx` without `curves.job_id` (`[LASTFIT]` with no `Chi2`) fails the automated audit.</Warning>
