This appendix lists the toolbar controls, item-type icons, tree row buttons, status badges, and menu icons displayed in the APS ELN Desktop window (<a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}/ch3-a-tour-of-the-main-window#the-three-panel-workspace`}>Chapter 3: The three-panel workspace</a>). Both light-theme and dark-theme variants are supplied in the runtime icon assets.

## Tree row buttons and status badges

When a row with children is selected in the **Projects** panel, the **Items** panel, or the **Hypotheses** list, three action buttons appear at the right end of that row, alongside any status or tag badges (<a href="#table-a-3">Table A-3</a>).

<p id="table-a-3" class="table-caption"><strong>Table A-3:</strong> Inline row buttons and tree badges.</p>

<DataTable
  headers={['Button or badge', 'Where it appears', 'Meaning and behavior']}
  rows={[
    ['**Collapse all below**', 'Right edge of selected parent row', 'Collapses every nested level under the selected row.'],
    ['**Expand all below**', 'Right edge of selected parent row', 'Expands every nested level under the selected row.'],
    ['**Focus**', 'Right edge of selected parent row', 'Tooltip: **Focus: collapse everything else**. Collapses all other branches and scrolls the selected row into view.'],
    ['**SIGNED** badge', 'Beside item title in Items panel', 'Dark-red rounded badge indicating that the item carries a cryptographic electronic signature and is permanently locked (<a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}/ch14-completeness-audit-and-signatures#signing-an-item-and-verifying-signature-integrity`}>Chapter 14</a>).'],
    ['**HYP** badge', 'Beside project or item title', 'Indicates hypotheses in a project (solid when a review decision is pending in Team mode) or an item attached as a hypothesis `Run` (<a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}/ch10-the-hypothesis-tree-and-evidence#attaching-runs-and-citing-evidence`}>Chapter 10</a>).'],
    ['**Tag** badge(s)', 'Beside item title in Items panel', 'Colored label(s) assigned via right-click > **Tags...** (<a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}/ch11-project-wiki-notes-tags-and-links#classifying-items-with-tags-and-favorites`}>Chapter 11</a>).'],
  ]}
/>
