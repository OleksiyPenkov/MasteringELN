Mandatory ELN Practice Assignment

Every student in the laboratory must complete two practice records in APS ELN by November 16, 2026. You will build Study A (261015A) in the Desktop GUI and Study B (261014B) using your AI agent with ELNMCPServer.exe and XRC_MCP.exe. Both practice projects will be audited automatically on the server after the deadline.

Step 1: Download the Raw Data Packages from the Lab Server

Download both raw instrument bundles from the CSMIC server and unpack them into two separate folders on your computer:

  1. Study A (261015A, BN/DLC tribological coating, 11 raw files): http://10.109.92.41/MasteringELN/data/Study-A-261015A-raw.zip. This archive includes .depo.json, .run.json, .tsv, three step-height .fibps scans, .mit nanoindentation, .zrw friction test, three wear-track .fibps scans, and checksums.md5.
  2. Study B (261014B, Co/C multilayer X-ray mirror, 7 raw files): http://10.109.92.41/MasteringELN/data/Study-B-261014B-raw.zip. This archive includes the 3 deposition files, 3 plasma-cleaning files, the raw reflectivity scan XRR-1_Co-C(261014B).xrdml, and checksums.md5. The .xrcx fit file is omitted so you can produce it by fitting the .xrdml curve in Task 2.

Step 2: Create Your Practice Group and Practice Projects

Log into APS ELN (ELabNotebook3.exe) with your personal laboratory account. Do not record practice data inside shared production projects.

  1. In the left Projects panel, choose Project > New Group (or right-click an empty area of the Projects panel and choose New Group…). Enter the exact group name Practice and click OK (Chapter 5: Creating, sorting, and moving projects and folders).
  2. Right-click your new Practice group and choose New Project…. Create your first practice project with the exact title Study A Practice and click Save.
  3. Right-click your Practice group a second time and choose New Project…. Create your second practice project with the exact title Study B Practice and click Save.

Remember

In the Commercial team edition, project groups are managed in the Desktop window. Creating Study B Practice inside your Practice group before launching your AI agent ensures both projects sit inside Practice under your user account.

Step 3: Complete Task 1 and Task 2

Task 1: Study A (261015A) in the Desktop GUI (Study A Practice)

Read Chapter 1 through Chapter 4: Your First Sample Record in Twenty Minutes and follow the four steps of Chapter 4 inside your Study A Practice project:

  1. Build the 6-item tree first: Deposition 261015A (Procedure) -> 261015A (Specimen) -> child actions Thickness 261015A, Indentation 261015A, and Friction test 261015A (with child action Wear track 261015A under Friction test 261015A).
  2. Attach the 11 raw files from Study-A-261015A-raw.zip to their matching items (Table 4-1).
  3. Extract all 23 Reader values using Extract Values and Extract values from 3 files…. Store 12 on Deposition 261015A, 3 on Thickness 261015A, 2 on Indentation 261015A, 3 on Friction test 261015A, and 3 on Wear track 261015A.
  4. Compute the 1 Derived value on Friction test 261015A with Add > Derived Value… (wear_rate_reciprocating, K=3.05×10−7 mm3/(N⋅mm)K = 3.05 \times 10^{-7}\,\text{mm}^3/(\text{N}\cdot\text{mm})K=3.05×10−7mm3/(N⋅mm)). Then right-click Deposition 261015A and run Check Value Sources… to verify zero drift across all 24 values.

Task 2: Study B (261014B) with Your AI Agent and XRC_MCP (Study B Practice)

Read Chapter 15 through Chapter 17: Study B: XRR Fitting with Provenance. Copy the 7 raw files from Study-B-261014B-raw.zip into your configured --upload-root directory and use your AI agent (ELNMCPServer.exe + XRC_MCP.exe) inside your Study B Practice project:

  1. Build the tree and attach the 7 raw files (Chapter 17: Part 1): Have the agent create folder 261014B inside Study B Practice and create Deposition 261014B (Proc), Cleaning 261014B (Action), 261014B (Specimen), and XRR-1 261014B (Action). Next, have it upload the 7 raw files and store the 18 Reader values (14 on Deposition 261014B and 4 on Cleaning 261014B) via extract_file_key_values and add_values.
  2. Fit XRR-1_Co-C(261014B).xrdml via XRC_MCP (Chapter 17: Part 2): Have the agent fit the reflectivity curve (SodaLimeGlass / [C/Co]×20[\text{C}/\text{Co}] \times 20[C/Co]×20 / surface C\text{C}C layer, polynomial order 1) in XRC_MCP.exe. Save the accepted .xrcx project using curves.job_id so [FITRESULT] and χ2\chi^2χ2 are written into the archive.
  3. Upload the .xrcx fit and store the 4 fit values: Have the agent create child action XRR fit 261014B under XRR-1 261014B and upload the saved .xrcx file. Finally, have it extract and store the 4 Reader values (Period, Co Thickness, C Thickness, and Chi2) via aps.xrcx.

Warning: never type Manual values to pass the check

Every stored number in both projects is checked for Reader or Derived provenance. Typing a number by hand (Manual source) or saving an .xrcx without curves.job_id ([LASTFIT] with no Chi2) fails the automated audit.
