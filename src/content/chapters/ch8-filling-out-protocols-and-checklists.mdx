Raw files record what the instrument measured, and extracted **Values** (<a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}/ch9-values-units-formulas-and-provenance#conditions-results-units-and-plausible-range-checks`}>Chapter 9</a>) store the numbers for plotting and search. Plenty of lab work, though, never shows up in a data file: how you cleaned and mounted the substrate, which target clamp you replaced, whether the cooling-water interlock passed, or why a coating looked hazy when you vented the chamber. **Protocols** record those checklists and setup notes right on the item.

## Adding and completing a protocol on an item

A protocol is a structured form (or free-text entry) attached to a **Procedure**, **Specimen**, or **Action** (<a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}/ch6-the-item-tree-and-experiment-templates#designing-a-clean-item-hierarchy`}>Chapter 6: Designing a clean item hierarchy</a>). APS ELN ships with ready-to-use protocol templates across twelve categories in `Assets/Runtime/ProtocolTemplates/`. These include **Deposition** (`CSMICSputtering.json`, `SputterIII_1Source.json`–`3Source.json`), **Characterization** (`XRD.json`, `XRF.json`, `SEM.json`, `TEM.json`, `AFM.json`, `3DProfiling.json`), and **Testing** (`Indentation.json`, `TribotestProtocol.json`, `Adhesion.json`), plus **Sample Preparation**, **Safety**, **Quality**, and **Reporting** (`WeeklyLabReport.json`).

Follow these steps to attach and complete a protocol:

1. Select the parent **Procedure**, **Specimen**, or **Action** in the **Items** panel.
2. Choose **Item > Add Protocol** (or press `Ctrl+Shift+P` in the team client).
3. In the **Select Template** dialog, pick a category and a template (or choose **(No template)** for a free-text protocol), then click **OK**.
4. In the **Protocol Editor**, check **Title**, **Protocol Type** (`Experiment`, `Measurement`, `Analysis`, `Synthesis`, `Testing`, `Note`, or `Other`), and **Validation Status** (`Draft`), and fill in the form fields.
5. Click **Save**. When you select the protocol in the **Items** panel, the **Content Viewer** renders the completed form as formatted HTML.

<Warning title="switching templates clears your entered form fields">Inside the **Protocol Editor**, changing the **Template** drop-down clears all saved form fields on that protocol so the new schema can load. Never switch the **Template** drop-down to browse other templates after you have typed experimental notes into a form.</Warning>

When you edit an existing protocol, an asterisk (`*`) marks unsaved changes. If you close the editor or click another item before saving, APS ELN prompts you to save, discard, or cancel so you never lose entries by accident.

## How protocol templates and AutoFill work

You can inspect or customize protocol templates by choosing **Reference Data > Protocol Templates...** (or **Templates > Protocol Templates...** in the team client). Each template is built from sections (**+Section**), rows (**+Row**), and typed fields (**+Field**), and can be tagged with equipment types via **Tags...** (<a href="#table-8-1">Table 8-1</a>).

When a protocol is created on an item that already holds values or metadata, the template's **AutoFill** bindings can pre-populate fields automatically (<a href="#table-8-1">Table 8-1</a>).

<Remember>Protocol form fields do not compute formulas or create `Derived` value provenance, and `AutoFill` inserts a one-time text snapshot when the protocol is created. Store quantitative results as item **Values** via **Extract Values** or **Add > Derived Value...** (<a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}/ch9-values-units-formulas-and-provenance#the-four-value-sources-and-derived-formulas`}>Chapter 9: The four value sources and Derived formulas</a>). Attach the protocol after those values are in place if your template uses `values.all`.</Remember>

<GoingDeeper title="protocol template versioning">

When you edit and save a protocol template that is already used by existing records, APS ELN automatically creates a **new template version** instead of overwriting the old schema. Existing protocols keep the exact field layout they were completed with, while new protocols use the latest active version. Tick **Show all versions (including inactive)** in the Template Manager to inspect historical versions.

</GoingDeeper>

## The protocol review workflow

Every protocol moves through four validation states (<a href="#fig-8-1">Figure 8-1</a>):

1. **`Draft`:** the author can edit all fields freely. When the form is complete, change **Validation Status** to `In Review` and click **Save**.
2. **`In Review`:** the protocol is locked against ordinary edits and enters the review queue.
3. **`Approved`:** the reviewer accepts the protocol. Once `Approved`, the protocol is permanently read-only and cannot return to `Draft`.
4. **`Rejected`:** the reviewer rejects the protocol and enters a mandatory rejection reason. The author reads the reason, switches the protocol back to `Draft`, saves, fixes the issue, and submits it to `In Review` again.

<Figure id="fig-8-1" number="8-1" src={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}/figures/fig-8-1-protocol-workflow.svg`} alt="State diagram showing protocol transitions from Draft to In Review, then branching either to Approved (Locked) or to Rejected (+ Reason) and returning to Draft" caption="The four validation states of a protocol. Submitting a Draft moves it to In Review. Approval locks the protocol permanently; Rejection records a required reason and lets the author return it to Draft." />

<InELN>To process protocols waiting in `In Review`, choose **Tools > Protocol Review...** (`Ctrl+Shift+R` in the team client). Select a protocol to read its rendered preview, then click **Approve** or **Reject**. Inside any saved protocol's editor, click **History...** to view the timestamped log of status changes, reviewers, and rejection comments.</InELN>

In **APS ELN Community**, protocol review is a local self-check by the scientist on their own notebook. Like all human review gates in APS ELN, it must be performed in the Desktop GUI (the MCP server cannot approve or reject protocols).

## Submitting protocols from the instrument in a team lab

In a team laboratory where instruments sit in a cleanroom or across the hall from your desk PC, you can complete a protocol right at the machine from your phone or tablet.

<InTeamLab title="the Quick Protocol mobile web app">

Laboratories running the Commercial **ELN Web API** server provide a mobile web app with three tabs (**Quick Protocol**, **Waiting for me**, and **Projects**). To record a run at an instrument in under a minute:

1. **Sign in** on your phone with your lab username and password (you can register your phone as a trusted web device so future visits sign in automatically).
2. **Find the specimen** by typing a few characters of its sample code (such as `261015A`) into the autocomplete box.
3. **Pick a template:** templates tagged for that instrument via **Tags...** and templates recently used on that specimen appear at the top of the list.
4. **Fill the fields, optionally snap a camera photo, and submit:** the protocol and photo are written directly to the shared MySQL and WebDAV server under that specimen. They appear immediately in the desktop client's **Protocols Awaiting Review** queue (<a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}/ch3-a-tour-of-the-main-window#the-dashboard-and-keyboard-navigation`}>Chapter 3: The Dashboard and keyboard navigation</a>).

</InTeamLab>
